Search

Query
Facebook adds loosely related items to bulk up your feed. This script filters out listings that don't actually match your query.
  • Listings must include every word of your query in their title, or they will be filtered out. A longer query will return narrower results.
  • Words match at their start, so "chev" catches both "Chevy" and "Chevrolet".
  • Add another query to search for multiple ways an item might be described. A listing is kept if its title has every word of at least one of your queries. Each query is a separate pass, so two queries take about twice as long as one.
✓Exact matching
Exact matching asks Facebook to be much stricter about matching your query, but often ends up excluding real results. It's useful if you want fast results, but in general, it's better to leave it off.

Cities

The search spans as far as your Facebook radius setting allows — up to 500 miles around each selected city. To get search results from the entire continental US, select all 12 cities and set your radius in Facebook to 500 miles.
Cities you add yourself are saved locally, and you can remove them by hovering over them.
How to get a city's link
  1. Open facebook.com/marketplace in your browser.
  2. Click the location on the left sidebar — it shows whatever city you're currently browsing.
  3. Type the city you want, pick it from the list, set the radius you want, and click Apply.
  4. Copy the full URL out of the address bar and paste it in the box above.

Email

Reports are sent from your own email account, so scheduled searches need this filled in before they can do anything. For Gmail you need an app password, not your normal password — see the steps below.
How to get a Gmail app password
  1. Go to myaccount.google.com and sign in.
  2. Open Security & sign-in in the left sidebar.
  3. Turn on 2-Step Verification if it isn't already. App passwords don't work without it.
  4. Use the search bar to find App passwords and open it.
  5. Type a name like Faceplace Marketbook and click Create.
  6. Google shows a sixteen-letter password. Copy it into the box above. You can include the spaces or leave them out.
